YOUR PORTFOLIO – HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE AND KEEP IT UP TO DATE
==========================================================

What is in this folder
  index.html          the page (you never need to edit this)
  config.js           ALL your text, links and artwork list  <- the only file you edit
  images/             your photos and artwork go here
  style.css, script.js, assets/   design and behaviour
  impressum.html, datenschutz.html   legal pages (German), fill in your details

1. Fill in your content
   Open config.js in any text editor (Notepad is fine). Change the text between the quotes.
   Put your pictures in the images folder and list them under "works" (see the examples in the file).

2. Look at it on your computer
   Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.

3. Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)
   a. Sign in at github.com and click New repository.
      Name it  yourusername.github.io  (replace yourusername with your GitHub name), set it to Public, click Create.
   b. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in EVERYTHING from this folder
      (the files and the folders assets and images, plus the hidden file .nojekyll if your computer shows it), then Commit.
   c. Open Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment" choose Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder / (root), Save.
   d. After a minute your site is live at  https://yourusername.github.io

4. Use your own free domain
   Settings > Pages > Custom domain: type your domain and Save.
   Then, at the place where you got the domain, add the DNS records GitHub shows you (the page tells you which).
   Tick "Enforce HTTPS" when it becomes available.

5. Add new artwork later
   Upload the image into the images folder on GitHub (Add file > Upload files), then edit config.js
   (click the file > pencil icon) and add one line to "works". Commit. Done.

Before you publish
  - Fill in impressum.html and datenschutz.html (German sites need an Impressum with a real address).
    The privacy text is only a starting point, not legal advice.
  - Your email address is visible in config.js and on the page. That is normal for a portfolio,
    but expect some spam. Use a separate email address for it if you like.
  - Make sure you have the right to publish every image.

Fonts: Inter, SIL Open Font License (assets/fonts/INTER-LICENSE.txt). Nothing is loaded from other websites.
The handwritten notes use the handwriting font your visitor's device has. To use one font everywhere,
see the commented block at the top of style.css.
